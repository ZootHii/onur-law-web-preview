Privacy and Personal Data Protection

This site

This site sets no cookies; it keeps no visitor statistics, contains no advertising or tracking tools and loads no code or fonts from other sites. There are no forms; the site asks you for no information.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.. GitHub Pages logs and stores the IP address of everyone who opens the site, for security purposes; GitHub, Inc. processes these logs under its own privacy statement. The GitHub, Inc. privacy statement

The address map loads from Google Maps (Google LLC) only when you click “Show the map”. From then on Google may process information such as your IP address and may use cookies; Google’s own privacy policy applies to that processing. Google privacy policy

On your first visit the site picks its language from your browser’s language setting. If you change the language, your choice is kept only in your own browser (localStorage) and is sent nowhere.

The phone, WhatsApp, e-mail, map and social media links on the site open the app or site concerned only when you click them; from then on, that service’s own terms apply.

The data processed when you get in touch

When you get in touch by phone, WhatsApp or e-mail, the following information you give is processed:

  • Identity details: your first name, surname and the identity details the matter needs
  • Contact details: your phone number, e-mail address and postal address
  • Details of the matter: your messages, notes of calls and the documents you send
  • Where the matter requires it, special categories of personal data — for example health data in a family case, or criminal record data in a criminal case

For the first meeting it is enough to share what is needed to understand the matter.

This information is collected directly from you: messages and e-mails electronically, by partly automated means; notes of phone calls by non-automated means, as part of the office’s file.

Purposes of processing

  • To answer your request and arrange a meeting
  • To assess your matter and whether the office can take it on
  • To conclude the engagement agreement and carry out cases and procedures under the power of attorney
  • To meet the obligations of the rules on the legal profession and of tax law
  • To establish, exercise or defend a right — for example the office defending its own rights in a dispute

Legal bases

Under the KVKK: processing directly related to concluding or performing a contract (Art. 5(2)(c)), a legal obligation (Art. 5(2)(ç)), establishing, exercising or protecting a right (Art. 5(2)(e)) and legitimate interest in answering your request, provided your fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed (Art. 5(2)(f)). For special categories of personal data: processing necessary to establish, exercise or protect a right (Art. 6(3)(d)), processing expressly provided for by law (Art. 6(3)(b)) and, where needed, your explicit consent (Art. 6(3)(a)).

Under the GDPR: a contract and steps taken before entering into it (Art. 6(1)(b)), a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) and legitimate interest in answering your request and in establishing or defending legal claims (Art. 6(1)(f)). For special categories of data: the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims (Art. 9(2)(f)).

Who receives the data

A lawyer may not disclose what they learn through their professional work (Turkish Attorneys Act No. 1136, Art. 36). Your data are passed on only as far as the matter requires, to:

  • Courts, enforcement offices, notaries and other public authorities — to carry out cases and procedures
  • The other party and their lawyer within a case or procedure — as far as the rules of procedure require
  • Sworn translators where needed — to translate documents
  • Public authorities empowered by law — when a legal obligation arises

Communication runs through the channel you choose: e-mails through Google’s Gmail service, WhatsApp messages through WhatsApp (Meta). The servers of these services may be outside Türkiye; if you prefer not to use them, you can call instead.

If you get in touch from the European Union: your data are processed at the office in Türkiye. The European Commission has no adequacy decision for Türkiye.

How long the data are kept

The data are kept as long as the purpose that requires them lasts: if the office does not take on the matter, until they are no longer needed after your request has been answered; if it does, for the duration of the mandate and afterwards until the retention periods set by law and the limitation periods for possible disputes have expired. At the end of these periods the data are erased, destroyed or anonymised (KVKK Art. 7).

Do you have to give the information?

You are not obliged to give it; without the information needed, however, your request cannot be assessed and the matter cannot be handled. No decision about you is based solely on automated processing, and no profiling takes place.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right, by applying to the data controller:

  • To learn whether your personal data are processed
  • To request information on it if they are
  • To learn the purpose of processing and whether the data are used accordingly
  • To know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom the data are transferred
  • To have incomplete or inaccurate data corrected
  • To have the data erased or destroyed under the conditions of Article 7 of the KVKK
  • To have the third parties to whom the data were transferred told of any correction, erasure or destruction
  • To object to a result against you that arises from analysis exclusively by automated systems
  • To claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing

If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the right (Arts. 15–21):

  • Of access to your data and to a copy of them
  • To rectification
  • To erasure
  • To restriction of processing
  • To object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • To data portability
  • To withdraw your consent at any time where processing is based on it — without affecting the processing before the withdrawal

Requests and complaints

You can send your request in writing to the address above, or by registered electronic mail (KEP), a secure electronic signature, a mobile signature or the e-mail address you gave the office before and that is in its records. A request names your first name, surname, Turkish identity number (for foreign nationals: nationality and passport number), your address for service and what you request; requests under the KVKK are made in Turkish (Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Applications to the Data Controller, Arts. 4–5).

Requests are concluded within thirty days at the latest (KVKK Art. 13); requests under the GDPR are answered within one month, which may be extended by two further months where necessary (GDPR Art. 12(3)). As a rule, no fee is charged for a request.

If your request is refused, the answer is insufficient or no answer comes in time, you may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board within thirty days of learning the answer, and in any case within sixty days of the request (KVKK Art. 14). Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu

If you are in the European Union, you may also complain to a supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work (GDPR Art. 77); in the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
