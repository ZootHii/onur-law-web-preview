Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working with the office.

Do I need to travel to Türkiye?

Most matters are handled under the power of attorney without travelling to Türkiye. If a hearing or a procedure needs you in person, you are told in advance.

In which language are meetings held?

Meetings are held in Turkish.

How does the first meeting take place?

You can call, write on WhatsApp or send an e-mail. A convenient time is agreed together; the meeting takes place by phone or video.

Where can I give the power of attorney?

In the Netherlands, at the Turkish Consulate General in Amsterdam, Deventer or Rotterdam by appointment, or at a Dutch notary (with an apostille). If you live in another country, the way that applies there is agreed at the first meeting.

How do I send documents?

You can send copies by e-mail. For documents needed in the original, you are told what to do.

How is the fee set?

The lawyer’s fee is set in a written fee agreement once the scope of the matter is clear.

How does the time difference work?

Office hours are in Turkish time. Türkiye is 1 hour ahead of the Netherlands in summer time and 2 hours ahead in winter time.
